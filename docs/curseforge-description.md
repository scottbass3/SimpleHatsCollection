# Simple Hats Collection

**An addon for [Simple Hats](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simplehats) that turns your
hats into a collection to complete.**

Simple Hats scatters hundreds of cosmetic hats across the world — but where do you *keep* them?
Simple Hats Collection gives every player a personal, unlimited **hat collection**: a place to store
every hat you find, see at a glance which ones you're still missing, and pull any hat back out whenever
you want to wear it.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎩 Store any hat in one click** — while it's in your inventory, hold **Shift** and **right-click** a
  hat to send it straight to your collection.
- **🎁 Open bags right into your collection** — **Shift + right-click** a Simple Hats hat bag and the
  random hat it rolls goes directly into your collection. A normal right-click still opens the bag the
  usual way.
- **📖 A collection screen** — press **K** (rebindable) to open your collection. One slot per hat:
  collected hats show their icon and how many you own, missing hats are greyed out so you always know
  what's left to find.
- **↩️ Take hats back anytime** — click a collected hat to return it to your inventory. Stored several
  variants or dyed versions of the same hat? A little pick-list lets you choose exactly which one to
  withdraw.
- **⭐ Favorites** — right-click a hat to favorite it. Favorites get a gold marker and float to the top
  of the grid.
- **🔎 Search & sort** — filter hats by name, and sort by default order, A–Z, rarity, or quantity
  collected. Hide the ones you haven't found yet to focus on your progress.
- **🗃️ Infinite, counted storage** — deposit as many copies as you like; each hat keeps a counter, and
  its variant and dye color are preserved exactly.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Open the collection | **K** (configurable in Controls) |
| Deposit a hat from your inventory | **Shift + Right-click** the hat |
| Deposit a bag's hat straight to the collection | **Shift + Right-click** the bag |
| Withdraw a hat | **Left-click** it in the collection |
| Favorite / unfavorite a hat | **Right-click** it in the collection |

## 🔧 Requirements

- **Minecraft 1.21.1**
- **[Simple Hats](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simplehats)** 0.4.0+
- **[Accessories](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/accessories)** (already required by Simple Hats)
- **NeoForge 21.1+** *or* **Fabric Loader + [Fabric API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api)**

## 💾 How your collection is saved

Your collection is stored **per player** and lives on the server (or your world in singleplayer). It
**survives death** and is kept safely server-side, so nothing is lost. Favorites are a personal
client-side preference and follow you across worlds.

## 🤝 Compatibility

Simple Hats Collection doesn't change how Simple Hats works — it only adds the collection and the
storing gestures. It works with any set of hats Simple Hats loads, including custom hats added through
Simple Hats' config.
